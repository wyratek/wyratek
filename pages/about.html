Our Story

One Range, One Partner,
for the Whole Workflow

Wyratek was founded on a simple idea: fabricators shouldn't have to juggle different suppliers for cutting, bending, rolling and pressing. By building a complete machine range, we make it easier to standardize production, training and spare parts.

Today our machines run in workshops across Europe, North America and beyond — from job shops to tier-one suppliers — supported by a team of engineers with decades of combined experience in metal forming equipment.
